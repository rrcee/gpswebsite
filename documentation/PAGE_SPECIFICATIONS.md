# PAGE SPECIFICATIONS

> Specifications for the core pages to guide design and development.

## Home Page
- **Purpose:** Welcome visitors, highlight key achievements, provide quick access to admissions and notices.
- **Target Audience:** Prospective parents, current parents, students.
- **Required Components:** Hero Slider, Principal’s Message, Quick Links, Notice Board Widget, Gallery Teaser, Footer.
- **SEO:** Title: Best School in Ernakulam | Global Public School; Desc: Discover world-class education...

## About Us
- **Purpose:** Establish trust and outline history.
- **Target Audience:** Parents, Staff, Community.
- **Required Components:** Timeline, Mission & Vision Cards, Leadership Profiles.

## Admissions
- **Purpose:** Convert prospective parents.
- **Target Audience:** Parents seeking admission.
- **Required Components:** Step-by-step accordion, Fee Table, CTA to Apply Now, Downloadable forms.
